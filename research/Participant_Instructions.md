# Participant Instructions (as shown on screen 4)

These instructions are displayed by the application before practice, with visual examples of each screen. They intentionally do **not** reveal the hypotheses.

---

## Your task: remember where the blue squares are

1. **Look at the cross (+)** in the middle of the screen. A grid will appear shortly.
2. **Memorise the pattern.** A 3×3 grid appears for half a second. **Four squares are blue.** Remember *where* they are.
3. **Middle phase (3 seconds).** Depending on the block, one of three things happens:
   - **Hold:** the screen is empty — just keep the blue pattern in mind.
   - **Equation:** a sum like `7 + 6 = 13` appears. Decide if it is correct.
     **F = True**, **J = False**.
   - **Symmetry:** a different grid with **orange** squares appears. Decide whether the orange pattern is symmetrical (the left side is a mirror image of the right side).
     **F = Yes (symmetrical)**, **J = No**.
   The orange grid is **not** the pattern you need to remember — keep remembering the blue one.
4. **Wait** for one more second.
5. **Compare.** A second blue grid appears. Is it **exactly the same** as the first blue pattern?
   - Press **F** if it is the **same**.
   - Press **J** if it is **different**.
   When it is different, exactly **one** blue square has moved to a new position. There are always four blue squares.
6. You have **2 seconds** to answer. Please respond **as quickly and as accurately as you can**.

## Keys

| Key | Recognition (blue grids) | Middle-phase task |
|---|---|---|
| **F** (left index finger) | Same | True / Yes |
| **J** (right index finger) | Different | False / No |

Keep your index fingers resting on **F** and **J** throughout.

## Structure

- **Practice:** 6 trials (2 of each type) with feedback. You can repeat the practice if you want.
- **Main task:** 3 blocks of 20 trials (about 2–3 minutes each), with a short break between blocks. No feedback is given in the main task.
- The task runs in **full-screen mode**. Please do not switch windows or tabs during the task.

## Tips

- Guessing is better than not answering, but please try your best on every trial.
- If you need to stop, press **Esc** and tell the researcher.
