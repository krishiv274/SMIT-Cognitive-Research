# USER GUIDE — Taking part in SMIT

This guide explains what participants experience. The researcher starts the task for you on the testing computer at **/participate**.

1. **Welcome.** Title of the study, what it is about, how long it takes (≈ 15–20 minutes) and how your privacy is protected.
2. **Information and consent.** Read the information sheet. Tick the box only if you agree; you can choose "I do not wish to take part" and nothing is stored.
3. **About you.** Enter your age (18+), gender (you may choose "Prefer not to answer"), academic course and email address. A participant ID (e.g. `P007`) is created for you. Your email is stored separately and never appears in results.
4. **Instructions.** Illustrated explanation of the task:
   - Look at the **+**.
   - Remember where the **4 blue squares** are (shown for half a second).
   - Middle phase (3 s): either nothing, an **equation** (F = true, J = false) or an **orange grid** — is it symmetrical? (F = yes, J = no). Do **not** remember the orange grid.
   - A second **blue** grid appears: **F = same**, **J = different** (one square moved). Answer within 2 seconds.
5. **Practice.** 6 trials with feedback. You may repeat the practice up to two times.
6. **Main task.** Three blocks of 20 trials in full-screen mode, no feedback, with a break of at least 20 seconds between blocks. Keep your index fingers on F and J.
7. **Completion.** You see your participant ID and confirmation that your data were saved. If the screen says data could not be saved, call the researcher and do not close the window.

**Stopping.** Press **Esc** at any time: after the current trial the task pauses and you can continue or stop and withdraw. You can also ask the researcher to delete your data later by quoting your participant ID.

**If the browser closes accidentally,** reopen `/participate` on the same computer and choose *Resume this session*; you continue from the first trial that was not saved.
